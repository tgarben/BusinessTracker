## Overview
Freelanced ("the app," "we," "us") is a time, mileage, expense, and income tracker for freelancers and small business owners, published by [Garben Technologies]. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it's protected.

**The short version:** Your data stays on your device and in your own private iCloud account. We do not operate servers that collect your data, we cannot see it, and we never sell or share it.

## Information the app handles
You enter and the app generates business records, including:

- **Time entries, mileage trips, expenses, income, clients, projects, invoices, and quotes** — along with details you add such as rates, amounts, notes, and your business and clients' billing information.
- **Location data** — when you track a trip or enable Automatic Mileage, the app records GPS coordinates and routes to calculate distance and start/end addresses.
- **Photos** — receipt images and any profile or business-logo image you add.
- **Motion data** — if you enable Automatic Mileage, the app uses motion activity to tell driving apart from walking so it only logs drives.

The app does **not** collect your contacts, browsing history, advertising identifiers, or any analytics about how you use it.

## Where your data is stored
- **On your device**, in the app's local storage.
- **In your private iCloud account** (Apple's CloudKit private database), if you're signed into iCloud, so your data syncs across your own devices and is backed up.

Because this uses Apple's iCloud and your device, **your data is controlled by you and your Apple ID. [Garben Technologies] has no server, no account system, and no access to your records.** Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

## Permissions and why we ask
The app only requests permissions for features you choose to use:

- **Location (While Using the App)** — to track a trip you start, and to fill in addresses from your current location.
- **Location (Always)** — *optional*, only if you turn on **Automatic Mileage**, so the app can detect and log drives in the background. It's off by default, and you can turn it off anytime in Settings.
- **Motion & Fitness** — *optional*, used with Automatic Mileage to distinguish driving from other movement.
- **Camera** — to photograph receipts and capture a profile or logo image.
- **Photos** — to attach images you select from your library.
- **Notifications** — for local reminders you opt into (e.g., tax deadlines, unpaid invoices, logged drives). These are generated on your device; we don't send push notifications from a server.

You can review or revoke any of these in the iOS **Settings** app at any time.

## Sharing your information
We do not sell, rent, or share your data. The only times information leaves the app are when **you** choose to:

- **Share or export** a document (invoice, quote, CSV, or backup) using the iOS share sheet — it goes wherever you send it (Mail, Messages, Files, etc.).
- **Sync via iCloud** — handled directly between your device and Apple under your Apple ID.

Some screens link to our other apps on the App Store; tapping a link opens the App Store and is subject to Apple's policies.

## Analytics & tracking
Freelanced contains **no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.** We do not track you across apps or websites.

## Data retention and deletion
- You can delete any record in the app. Deleted items go to **Recently Deleted** for 30 days, then are permanently removed.
- **Deleting the app** removes its local data from your device.
- Data stored in **iCloud** is managed through your Apple ID — you can remove it via iOS Settings → your name → iCloud, or by signing out of iCloud.
- You can export a full backup of your data at any time from Settings.

## Children
Freelanced is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Third-party services
- **Apple iCloud / CloudKit** — used to store and sync your data under your Apple ID. See Apple's Privacy Policy at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.
